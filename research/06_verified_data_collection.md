# Demonstration data collection notes

`data/verified_distributors.csv` contains 30 US distributors/resellers selected from official public websites. It is a **demonstration research dataset**, retrieved 2026-11-25. Each record preserves its public official source URL, explicitly marks unknown fields, and contains no personal contact data.

Verification means the organization and broad operating category were confirmed from the cited public corporate site—not that every portfolio, scale, or territory attribute was independently audited. This conservative definition is intentional: a production research job must acquire field-level evidence and allow reviewer approval.

`synthetic_demo_accounts.csv` is separately labelled synthetic and must never be used as research evidence, outbound recipients, or commercial reporting.
