# ICP and distributor persona research

## ICP templates

| Template | Target | Must-have | Helpful signals | Disqualify |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Consumer-electronics regional distributor | US regional distributors/resellers | documented CE portfolio, named service territory, B2B/retail capability | new vendor line, warehouse expansion, retailer relationship | marketplace-only with no B2B evidence; direct competitor conflict |
| Gaming & accessories channel partner | gaming/hardware distributors | gaming/peripherals portfolio, e-commerce or specialty-retail reach | launch/news pages, esports or retail partner activity | adult/gambling-only catalog; no US delivery/coverage evidence |
| Sports & recreation wholesale partner | specialty sporting goods distributors | adjacent category, dealer/wholesale program, regional footprint | new brands, new category page, trade-show presence | pure manufacturer with no distribution service evidence |

## Required evidence to qualify

1. Official website and company identity; 2. geography served; 3. category or brands; 4. distribution/wholesale capability; 5. conflict review. Missing evidence lowers **confidence**, not fit.

## Market configuration

US is the pilot. The data model stores ISO market, permitted sources, applicable law profile, languages, and sending mode. Initial supported configuration records can include US, Canada, UK, EU/EEA, and India, but this is architecture readiness—not a claim of operational launch or legal clearance.
