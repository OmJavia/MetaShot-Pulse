# Competitor analysis and positioning

| Product | Strengths | Automation / AI | Public integration evidence | MVP lesson / gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clay | provider waterfall, flexible tables, enrichment | Claygent, AI research, scoring | 75+ enrichment tools; CRM/webhook/HTTP API | powerful but workflow-builder oriented; lacks channel-native evidence schema |
| Apollo | people/company data, search, sequencing | assistant and AI insights | API supports search, enrichment, records, sequences; plan/credits vary | use as opt-in enrichment adapter, not system of record |
| Unify | signal-led outbound plays | first/third-party/AI-discovered signals | 40+ source claim; product-signal integrations | signals are useful input, but category/portfolio validation is separate |
| Common Room | identity resolution and buyer signals | RoomieAI research, scoring | CRM, data warehouse, engagement integrations | expensive signal layer; no distributor-fit ontology |
| HubSpot | CRM, enrichment, intent, automation | Breeze data/intent agents | CRM API, enrichment and buyer-intent features | sync qualified accounts; do not reimplement CRM |
| ZoomInfo | enterprise B2B intelligence | intent and enrichment | APIs/availability are contract dependent | benchmark, but budget and provenance opacity are concerns |

## Evidence-based positioning

ChannelSignal sits **before and alongside the CRM**: it is the accountable research and decision layer for channel partner selection. It should accept provider data, but its durable value is the evidence ledger, category-aware rubric, reviewer corrections, and reproducible decision history.

## Explicit MVP exclusions

Skip contact database replication, multi-channel automatic sending, anonymous visitor deanonymization, ad activation, and proprietary intent-data acquisition. Implement import, account research, qualification, review, draft, pipeline, analytics, and connector stubs first.

Pricing/current packaging is volatile; official references are maintained in `source_references.md`.
