# Market research — ChannelSignal

## The operational problem

Physical-product manufacturers expanding through distribution must answer a different question from typical SaaS prospecting: **can this company move this category in this geography, and what evidence supports that conclusion?** Existing GTM stacks find people and activity, but rarely model channel capability, portfolio conflict, territory overlap, or source freshness together.

ChannelSignal is therefore a channel-intelligence system, not a generic CRM. It converts public, lawful company evidence into explainable distributor-fit decisions; keeps unknowns explicitly unknown; and routes only review-approved, compliant outreach.

## Market observations

- Modern GTM engineering combines CRM hygiene, enrichment, lead routing, workflow automation, experiments, dashboards, and AI agents. Current roles demand end-to-end ownership rather than reporting alone (see `02_ai_gtm_hiring_analysis.md`).
- Clay, Apollo, Common Room, Unify, HubSpot and ZoomInfo provide strong horizontal data, signal, or workflow primitives. They do not make a channel-specific evidence model their primary unit of work.
- For distributors, public sources are fragmented: company portfolio pages, vendor line cards, locations, retailer pages and association directories. Evidence must remain attached to the account and field.

## Product wedge

1. **Evidence ledger:** every material claim has URL, excerpt, captured time, source type and reviewer status.
2. **Two-axis qualification:** fit (commercial match) is never conflated with evidence confidence.
3. **Channel graph:** model category, brands, coverage, retail/e-commerce capability, and conflicts—not just contacts.
4. **Safe activation:** research and drafts are automatic; messages are review-only and simulation-first.

## MVP non-goals

No LinkedIn scraping, guessed emails, autonomous sending, opaque “AI score,” full CRM replacement, or claims of pipeline lift based on demo data.
